## Main Idea

2 players. 2 individuals or against AI levels

### Royale plan

Each turn Players will be presented with 3 cards.
3 cards will contain different or same type of cards.

Cards are categories in the following types - 
1. Damage : -1.5x, -1x, 0x, 1x, 2x ( negative number means multiplier  reduce damage to opponent)
2. Projectile-Spell : 25, 50, 100, 200, 500 (Direct damage to opponent)
3. Epic-Spell : Heal, Poison, Sleep, Hex, Revoefil 

Note: Hex reverses magic effects. Like poison falls on user itself, Heal deals damage to user. Basically user becomes the target.
Note: Revoefil is instant-death to opponent. If Hex is active, user dies.

3 cards can be of any combinations of these - 
like - 
* Card 1 = Projectile-Spell: 50; card 2 = Damage: -1.5x; card 3 = Projectile-Spell: 500
* card 1 = Epic-Spell: Poison; card 2 = Damage: 1x; card 3 = Projectile-Spell: 50
* card 1 = Projectile-Spell: 50; card 2 = Damage:  -1.5x; card 3 = Damage: 2x

### The twist

Each turn the card is randomly picked by game engine. However, your opponent or AI will know the hidden face of the cards. So the opponent can tell a truth or lie as a hint. It's upto the player to choose.

For AI, some plan s are in motion for making additional characters like 1. Devil's advocate and 2. Guardian Angle.

Devil's Advocate has Higher "chance" to lie. It's not 100% liar. Randomly decided.
Guardian Angle may fall in love with the Devil and try to sabotage your turn by lying once in a while. (Each turn random chance of increase love for devil which will increase the mysterious. Angel's love-meter will be visible)
